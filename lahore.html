Lahore – City of Gardens

Historical Wonders

Lahore is the cultural heartbeat of Pakistan, distinguished by its magnificent Mughal architecture. Key landmarks include the monumental Badshahi Mosque, the sprawling historic Lahore Fort, and the serene layouts of the Shalimar Gardens.
