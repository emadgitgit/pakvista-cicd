Swat – Switzerland of Pakistan

Lush Valleys and Cultural Sites

Swat Valley features alpine meadows, rushing rivers, and bustling regional craft markets. Tourists visit to ski at Malam Jabba, shop traditional textiles in Mingora Bazaar, and explore Buddhist heritage at the Swat Museum.
