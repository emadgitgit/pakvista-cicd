Hunza Valley – Paradise on Earth

Alpine Majestic Landmarks

Located in Gilgit-Baltistan, Hunza is famous for its soaring snow peaks, crystal-clear blue lakes, and historical forts. Visitors travel from around the globe to admire the Rakaposhi viewpoint, cruise across Attabad Lake, and wander through Baltit Fort.
